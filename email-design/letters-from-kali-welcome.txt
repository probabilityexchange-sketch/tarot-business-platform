Welcome. I’m glad you’re here.

Letters from Kali brings together writing, tarot, and spiritual reflection: ways of exploring what we notice, the meanings we give it, and the stories we’re living.

I hold a master’s degree in creative writing from Goddard College and an undergraduate degree in psychology from the University of Tennessee. Writing belongs at the heart of this work—an invitation to observe closely and ask honest questions.

The intuition guide is sent separately after you confirm your subscription. In the letters that follow, we’ll explore discernment: how to listen to an impression while examining fear, desire, and the need to be certain.

If you’d like a place to begin, take a page and write:

One thing I’m carrying right now is…

One question I keep returning to is…

One thing I’d like to understand more clearly is…

You don’t need a finished answer to begin a conversation.

These emails will also carry invitations to readings, new writing, and gatherings as they take shape. You can unsubscribe whenever you wish.

What brought you here? You’re welcome to reply with a few words, a question, or a little of your story.

Kali
