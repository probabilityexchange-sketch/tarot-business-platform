ON DISCERNMENT · LETTER 02

What is fear protecting?

A strong feeling of “no” deserves attention.

It may be responding to something happening now. It may also be connected to an old experience, an anticipated disappointment, or the discomfort of doing something unfamiliar.

The useful question is: What is this fear responding to?

A MOMENT ON THE PAGE

Take a page and write:

I am afraid that…

Be specific. Continue with:

What I know right now is…

Then ask:

What would help me understand this better?

Perhaps you need information, a clearer boundary, more time, or a conversation. If there is a concrete risk, address it.

There is no need to force yourself into a “yes” to prove that you trust your intuition. You also don’t have to treat every uncomfortable feeling as a final answer.

You might be able to take a small, reversible step and see what you learn. You might decide to wait.

Both can be thoughtful choices.

What question would help you understand a fear you’re carrying today? You’re welcome to write back.
