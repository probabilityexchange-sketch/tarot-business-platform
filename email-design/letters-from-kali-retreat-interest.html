A GATHERING IN THE MAKING

What would make room for you?

I’m exploring an overnight retreat in North Georgia for April 2027, bringing writing and spiritual reflection together.

The questions behind these letters would be part of it: how we listen to ourselves, how we examine fear and desire, and how writing can give us room to consider what we’re noticing.

North Georgia is the region I’m considering. The venue, exact dates, format, and price are still being worked out. I’d like to hear from people who might want to come before those decisions are made.

A MOMENT ON THE PAGE

If this interests you, consider:

What would I most like to explore through writing and reflection?

Would I want quiet writing time, guided exercises, shared conversation, or a mixture?

Where would I be traveling from, and what travel distance would be realistic?

What practical details would make it possible for me to attend?

You can also share what price range you would be comfortable considering, if you wish.

There is room to help shape this before the plans are final.

Reply with whatever you’d like me to know. An expression of interest is not a reservation or a commitment to attend.

When there is a clear plan, I’ll share the details here.
