About Kali

Kali is a gifted intuitive reader, spiritual counselor, and guide based in Chattanooga, Tennessee. With years of experience working with tarot, oracle cards, and Lenormand, Kali offers readings that are grounded, compassionate, and deeply transformative.

Kali’s approach blends traditional card reading with intuitive channeling, creating sessions that feel both ancient and uniquely personal. Each reading is a collaborative exploration — not a prediction, but a conversation with the wisdom already within you.

Whether you are navigating a life transition, seeking clarity on relationships or career, or simply curious about what the cards have to say — Kali welcomes you.
