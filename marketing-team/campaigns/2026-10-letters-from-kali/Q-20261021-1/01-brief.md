# Brief: Welcome + three-part discernment guide email

## Assignment
- Channel: email, first step of the Brevo welcome automation, sent right after signup.
- Audience: new free-guide subscribers, reflective professionals in transition [owner].
- Goal: deliver the promised free three-part discernment guide and set up the weekly Letters.
- CTA: primary is "reply" (Kali's existing pattern). Optional soft close: "Book a Reading" -> https://kalimeister.com/readings [owner], followed by the reading disclaimer.
- Length ~600-900 words. Same layout as the Letters (eyebrow, H1, body, "A MOMENT ON THE PAGE" boxes, pull quote, sign-off "Kali").

## Facts you may use
- Letters from Kali offers a free three-part discernment guide, then weekly letters [src: https://kalimeister.com/letters].
- Kali Meister is a writer and spiritual practitioner in Tennessee [src: https://kalimeister.com/about].
- Readings are private and online; CTA "Book a Reading" -> https://kalimeister.com/readings [owner].
- Reading disclaimer (exact): "Tarot readings are offered for reflection and personal insight. They don't predict outcomes and aren't a substitute for medical, mental health, legal or financial advice." [owner]
- Welcome lines already approved in the revised welcome (Q-20261017-1): "Welcome. I'm glad you're here.", "Letters from Kali brings together writing, tarot, and spiritual reflection...", "You don't need a finished answer to begin a conversation." [src: Q-20261017-1/04-candidate.md]
- Letters 01-05 cover: noticing vs interpreting (01), fear (02), desire/ego (03), revising an impression (04), boundaries (05) [src: candidates].

## Voice for this piece
- Calm, reflective, plain; short declarative sentences; invites rather than urges [owner].
- Tarot as reflection on a question, never prediction [src: about].
- Personal letter feel, first person as Kali.
- Banned: manifest, destiny, guaranteed, unlock, "transform your life", therapy [owner]; also avoid psychic, third eye, energy, chakra, downloads, "predict" (except to say tarot doesn't).

## Suggested guide shape (writer may improve)
- Part 1: Notice (separate what you felt from the story you told about it).
- Part 2: Question (ask what hope, fear or wish is shaping the impression).
- Part 3: Take one small step and return to the page (revisit what you learned).
- Tarot touch: draw one card as a question to sit with, not an answer.
Keep each part short so Letters 01-05 can go deeper without repeating the exact prompts.

## Claim limits
- Never claim: predictions or outcomes; guaranteed results; health, mental health, legal or financial benefits (no "heal", "anxiety", "clarity guaranteed"); credentials, years of practice, client counts, testimonials [owner].
- Must include: footer with Kali Meister and 11265 Hallett Street, Soddy Daisy, TN 37379, and unsubscribe link [owner]; reading disclaimer if readings are mentioned [owner].

## Not facts: do not state these as true
- That the subscriber "confirmed" a subscription (double opt-in is undecided) [gap]: don't mention confirmation.
- Weekly cadence is set in Brevo (7 days) [owner, Billy approved the automation]: "a letter each week" is fine.
- Retreat/gatherings: not open [src: shop]; don't mention dates.
