# Work Order: Short-form scripts for the 22 Major Arcana cards

## Request (verbatim)
> "I want to have a bunch of reels and short form content about the different tarot cards." / "yes, draft the scripts and let me run them by kali. then we'll figure out the artwork."

## Goal
A ready-to-film series of short videos, one per Major Arcana card, that introduces Kali's reflective approach to tarot and grows her audience toward the free guide and readings.

## Success metric
Follows, saves, comments and profile visits on TikTok; free-guide signups from the bio link. [assumption]

## Audience
Reflective professionals and high-achievers in transition [owner], plus curious tarot beginners on TikTok [assumption].

## Offer and CTA
Soft CTA only: "free guide, link in bio" (Free three-part discernment guide, kalimeister.com/letters). No booking CTA in the videos (no open slots as of 2026-10-17) and no prices.

## Deliverables
| # | Asset | Channel | Owner role | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Script pack: cards 0-10 (The Fool to Wheel of Fortune) | TikTok first; repost YouTube Shorts + Facebook Reels | content-producer | this run |
| 2 | Script pack: cards 11-21 (Justice to The World) | same | content-producer | this run |

## Format per card
- Card number and name; working title.
- HOOK (first 2 seconds, spoken + on-screen text, under 12 words).
- SCRIPT for Kali to say on camera, 30-45 seconds (about 80-110 words): what the card shows, what it traditionally points to, framed as something to reflect on, never a prediction.
- REFLECTION QUESTION to close (also as on-screen text).
- SHOT NOTES (1-2 lines; artwork TBD, so describe "card shown" generically).
- CAPTIONS: TikTok caption (<150 chars + 3-5 hashtags); YouTube Shorts title (<70 chars, searchable, includes "tarot card meaning"); Facebook caption (1-2 personal sentences).
- Same CTA line at the end of each caption: "Free guide in bio."

## Constraints
- Kali films herself on TikTok [owner]; scripts must be easy to say naturally. Plain spoken English, short sentences.
- Reflection, not prediction. No "this card means you will...", no outcome promises, no health/legal/financial claims [owner]. Death, Tower, Devil etc. handled calmly, no fear-baiting.
- Card meanings: describe widely known traditional symbolism in general terms; do not quote any guidebook text; do not name or describe a specific copyrighted deck's artwork (artwork is undecided).
- Banned words per voice.md; no "manifest", "destiny", "unlock", "guaranteed"; avoid "psychic", "energy" claims.
- No credentials, years of practice, client stories.
- Series consistency: same structure every video; vary hooks.

## Assumptions made at intake
- Major Arcana first (22 cards); Minor Arcana later. [assumption]
- Kali on camera, not an AI avatar. [inference from "she's going to do tiktok"]
- Artwork decided later; scripts are deck-agnostic.
