## 0. The Fool
- **Hook (Spoken):** "The Major Arcana opens at the edge of a cliff."
- **On-Screen Text (Hook):** The first card is standing at a cliff edge
- **Spoken Script:**
  This is The Fool, card zero. It's often shown as a traveler at the edge of that cliff, a small bag over one shoulder, a dog close by. Traditionally, it points to beginnings, openness, and taking a step before you have the whole map. It can also point to carelessness, so I read it as a question about how you're standing at your own edge. Are you curious? Are you rushing? Or are you waiting to feel completely sure first?
- **Closing Question (Spoken + On-Screen):** "Where are you standing at the edge of something right now?"
- **Shot Note:** Waist-up, natural light. Hold up card on "often shown as."

## 1. The Magician
- **Hook (Spoken):** "Everything this card needs is already on the table."
- **On-Screen Text (Hook):** It's already on the table
- **Spoken Script:**
  The Magician is often shown standing at a table with four objects in front of them: a cup, a sword, a wand and a coin. Those are the four suits of the tarot. One hand points up, the other points down. Traditionally, it's a card about skill, focus and picking up the tools already in front of you instead of waiting for new ones. The shadow side is all talk and no follow-through. So when this card comes up, I look at the table first. What's already there that I keep overlooking?
- **Closing Question (Spoken + On-Screen):** "What's on your table that you haven't picked up yet?"

- **On-Screen Text (Hook):** The card that listens
