# Brief: Major Arcana short-form scripts, cards 11-21 (Justice, The Hanged Man, Death, Temperance, The Devil, The Tower, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, Judgement, The World)

## Assignment
- 11 scripts, one per card, in the per-card format in the Work Order. TikTok first (Kali on camera), reposted to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.
- Goal: show Kali's reflective take on each card; build follows and guide signups.
- CTA: captions end "Free guide in bio." Nothing else.

## Facts you may use
- Kali Meister, writer and spiritual practitioner [src: https://kalimeister.com/about].
- Her stance: "Tarot is not about predicting the future — it's about illuminating the present." [src: https://kalimeister.com/about] (may be paraphrased; do not attribute as a quote in captions unless verbatim)
- Free three-part discernment guide at kalimeister.com/letters [src].
- Traditional, widely known meanings of the Major Arcana (general knowledge). Describe in your own words; no guidebook quotes.

## Voice for this piece
- Calm, reflective, plain; short spoken sentences; invites rather than urges [owner].
- Each card is a question to look at your present, not a forecast.
- Hooks can be curious or gently surprising (e.g. correcting a common fear), never clickbait or fear-baiting.
- Banned: manifest, destiny, guaranteed, unlock, "transform your life", therapy [owner]; avoid psychic, energy, vibration, "the universe is telling you".

## Claim limits
- Never: predictions ("you will", "is coming"), outcome promises, health/mental health/legal/financial claims, credentials, client stories [owner].
- Readings not promoted in these videos, so no disclaimer needed in captions; if a script mentions readings, drop it.

## Not facts: do not state these as true
- Audience pains beyond "unsure whether a strong feeling is fear, ego, desire or intuition" [inference].
- Any specific deck's imagery (artwork undecided) [gap]: describe the card's common symbols generically ("often shown as...").

## Unknowns
- Artwork/deck [gap]; posting cadence [gap]; Kali's sign-off on interpretations [gap] (flag cards where interpretation choices matter most).
