## Plain-Text Version

WRITING · TAROT · SPIRITUAL REFLECTION
Letters from Kali

THE COLLECTION · NOW OPEN

A small table for the writing hour.

Hello friend,

When you spend enough time with notebooks, tarot cards, and half-finished drafts, you start to notice the small things that keep you company at the desk. A favorite mug. A pencil that stays sharp. A light beside the page when the house is still quiet.

Today, I am opening a small shop on the site: The Kali Meister Collection.

It holds two sets of everyday objects made for people who live inside stories, questions, and transitions, and who don't mind a little dry humor along the way.

Explore the collection: https://kalimeister.com/shop

---

1. THE TWELVE LITERARY ZODIAC TEES ($34)

Every sign has a way of approaching a blank page, a five-year plan, or a card they weren't expecting. We printed all twelve on soft, everyday shirts, each with a single line in the voice of the sign:

- Aries: "I started before I finished reading the instructions."
- Taurus: "My comfort zone has excellent lighting."
- Gemini: "Both versions of me have a point."
- Cancer: "I brought snacks and emotional context."
- Leo: "Even my footnotes deserve applause."
- Virgo: "Trust the universe. Proofread anyway."
- Libra: "I see both sides. That’s the problem."
- Scorpio: "You’ve read the cover. Not the manuscript."
- Sagittarius: "I went looking for meaning. I missed my flight."
- Capricorn: "My five-year plan has a sequel."
- Aquarius: "The plot was predictable. I changed the genre."
- Pisces: "I was listening. To the ocean in my head."

---

2. THE SIX INTENTION CANDLES ($28)

A candle doesn't answer a question for you. What it does is mark a beginning. Strike a match, open a notebook, and give yourself twenty minutes where nothing has to be solved all at once.

Each of the six candles features a vintage library checkout card label on an amber apothecary jar, complete with a catalog call number, a wry borrower's margin note, and a tilted rubber stamp:

- Peace (Call No. BF 1729 .P4): "I have closed all my mental tabs." (Olive branch, quiet pause, and room to breathe · Stamped: QUIET PLEASE)
- Clarity (Call No. PN 145 .C5): "The fog lifted. I still have questions." (Still water, morning light, and a clean page · Stamped: SEE MARGIN NOTES)
- Protection (Call No. Z 1003 .P7): "Do not disturb the narrator." (Oak wreath, closed door, and clear boundaries · Stamped: DO NOT DISTURB)
- Prosperity (Call No. Z 471 .P8): "Funding my future bookshop era." (Pomegranate, full shelves, and good ink · Stamped: BOOKSHOP FUND)
- Love (Call No. HQ 801 .L6): "Romancing my own peace of mind." (Flowering stems, warm tea, and self-kindness · Stamped: RENEWED)
- The Writing Hour (Call No. PN 171 .W7): "Do not interrupt a sentence in progress." (Blank page, open notebook, and late light · Stamped: DRAFT IN PROGRESS)

---

A MOMENT ON THE PAGE

Whether or not you pick up a candle or a shirt today, here is a small prompt for your notebook this week:

Look at the twelve zodiac lines above and set your birth chart aside for a minute. Which line sounds most like the chapter you are living right now? Write that sentence at the top of a clean page, and give yourself five minutes to write what comes after it.

If you would like to browse the shirts and candles, the shop is open here:
Shop the Collection -> https://kalimeister.com/shop

And if you are sitting with a question or a decision that wants a longer conversation, I offer private online tarot readings. Each one is a reading with room for you.

Tarot readings are offered for reflection and personal insight. They don't predict outcomes and aren't a substitute for medical, mental health, legal or financial advice.

Warmly,
Kali
