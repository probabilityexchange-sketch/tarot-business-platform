## Always
- Frame readings as reflection on a question, a decision or a story. [src: https://kalimeister.com/readings]
- Stay inside claims-and-compliance.md. [owner]

## Never
- Predict outcomes or promise results. [owner]
- Call readings "therapy". [owner]

## Banned words and patterns
- manifest, destiny, guaranteed, "unlock", "transform your life", "therapy". [owner]
- Em dashes and the patterns listed in the no-ai-slop skill, in new copy. [assumption]
