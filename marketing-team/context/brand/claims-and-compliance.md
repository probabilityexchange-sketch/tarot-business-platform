> **The one question:** What must never appear in your marketing, and what must always appear?

The owner adopted these rules as policy on 2026-10-17. [owner] No legal review has happened. [gap]

## Never claim
- Predictions of events or outcomes. [owner]
- Guaranteed results or outcome promises. [owner]
- Health, mental health, legal or financial claims, including grief-healing. [owner]
- That readings are "therapy". [owner]
- "5,000 readings", "40+ years", degrees, hospice counselor, Reiki Master, channeling since 2008, until Kali confirms. [owner]
- Client stories (real or composite) and testimonials, until Kali supplies approved ones. [owner]
- Banned words: manifest, destiny, guaranteed, "unlock", "transform your life". [owner]
- That the shop, resources, Gatherings or TikTok Live are open. [src: https://kalimeister.com/shop]

## Allowed with conditions
| Claim type | Condition |
|---|---|
| Testimonials | Real, attributed, with permission on file. None exist. [gap] |
| Numbers/stats | Must have a source in frontmatter. |
| Credentials | Kali confirms first. [owner] |
| AI-avatar video | Disclosed as AI-generated. [owner] Kali's approval of avatar use: [gap] |
| Prices and policy | Only as in offers.md. [owner] |

## Required disclaimers

|---|---|
